# 🇮🇳 Bharat Sales Intelligence

### Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

Bharat Sales Intelligence is a modern, interactive web-based dashboard designed to analyze sales and revenue data and transform it into meaningful business insights.

The dashboard helps users monitor important KPIs, analyze revenue trends, compare products and regions, and explore sales performance through interactive visualizations.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The project demonstrates how data visualization and business intelligence techniques can be used to understand sales performance.

Users can explore sales data through interactive charts, filters, KPIs, and transaction records to identify important business trends and performance indicators.

The dashboard uses Indian Rupee (₹) formatting to provide an Indian business-oriented sales analysis experience.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📈 Revenue Trend Analysis
- 💰 Total Revenue Tracking
- 💎 Total Sales Analysis
- 🛍️ Total Orders
- 📦 Units Sold
- ✨ Average Order Value
- 🏆 Top Performing Products
- 🍩 Category-wise Sales Analysis
- 🌍 Regional Sales Analysis
- 💠 Revenue by Region
- 📅 Monthly Sales Trends
- ⚖️ Sales vs Revenue Comparison
- 🧠 Automatic Business Insights
- 📋 Detailed Transaction Table
- 🎛️ Interactive Filters
- 🖱️ Clickable and Highlightable Charts
- 🇮🇳 Indian Rupee (₹) Currency Formatting

---

## 📂 Data Import

The dashboard supports importing sales data from:

- 📄 CSV files
- 📊 Excel files (.xlsx, .xls)
- 🗄️ SQLite databases (.db, .sqlite, .sqlite3)

Imported data is processed in the browser and automatically used to update the dashboard.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard using:

- Category
- Region
- Product
- Start Date
- End Date

All KPIs, charts, insights, and transaction records update automatically according to the selected filters.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes multiple interactive visualizations:

| Visualization | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 📈 Revenue Journey | Analyze revenue over time |
| 🍩 Category Performance | Compare sales contribution by category |
| 🏆 Top Performing Products | Identify the best-performing products |
| 🌍 Regional Sales | Compare sales across regions |
| ⚖️ Sales vs Revenue | Compare sales and revenue |
| 📦 Product Quantity | Analyze units sold by product |
| 💠 Revenue by Region | Analyze regional revenue distribution |
| 📅 Monthly Sales | Identify monthly sales patterns |

---

## 🖱️ Interactive Graph Highlighting

The dashboard provides clickable charts.

Users can click on individual graph elements such as:

- Revenue points
- Product bars
- Category segments
- Regional values
- Monthly sales points

The selected element is highlighted and its corresponding value is displayed below the chart.

This makes the dashboard more interactive and user-friendly.

---

## 🧠 Business Insights

The dashboard automatically generates important business insights such as:

- 🏆 Best-performing product
- 💜 Leading sales category
- 🌎 Strongest region by revenue

These insights allow users to quickly understand important business trends.

---

## 💰 Indian Currency Support

All sales and revenue values are displayed using Indian Rupee (₹) formatting.

Examples:

- ₹1,50,000
- ₹12,50,000
- ₹1,25,00,000

This makes the dashboard suitable for analyzing Indian sales and revenue data.
